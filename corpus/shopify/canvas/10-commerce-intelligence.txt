Legend
  [R] reported: in a filing, or computed directly from filing figures
  [S] stated by management on a call or in narrative; not independently verified
  [I] inferred by us; rests on an assumption or interpretation
  [X] claim that the corpus contradicts
  a -[D:grade]-> b  dependency: b needs or is fed by a
  a -[C:grade]-> b  sequence: a led to or preceded b
  a -[O:grade]-> b  control: a holds or manages b
  a -[M:grade]-> b  structure: a belongs to or connects with b
  grades: mech (true by arithmetic or definition) | causal (controlled test; none found)
          assoc (figures move together) | data (management figure, unverifiable)
          claim (asserted, no figure) | untestable (corpus lacks the data)
          contra (contradicted by the corpus)
  => NN  detail lives on canvas NN | n/d  not disclosed | bp  basis points
