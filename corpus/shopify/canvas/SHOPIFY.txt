SHOPIFY CANVAS SET
Corpus: corpus/shopify, FY2018 filings through the Q3 2026 earnings call.
Perspective: long-horizon strategy. Numbers are read as allocation decisions
inside one interdependent company, and unsuccessful initiatives are read as
moves within that system, without assuming that adaptation excuses the cost.

4. LEVELS

Generic levels, for any company filing:
  G1 Thesis           what the company believes about the world
  G2 Value engine     the reinforcing loop it claims
  G3 Declared themes  what management says it prioritizes each year [S]
     Strategic domains  lasting areas that compete for priority [I]
  G4 Capability       what each domain offers
  G5 Initiative       a dated move, with a return horizon and lifecycle stage
  G6 Evidence         a figure or statement supporting a node

Separate axes:
  Reported structure  company -> segment -> revenue line, plus costs by function
  Time                era -> fiscal year -> quarter

Shopify's levels on these canvases:
  S1 Thesis      01
  S2 Flywheel    01, in three versions
  S3 Domains     03-10
  S4-S5          the branches within each domain canvas
  S6 Evidence    the tagged leaves

Eras, used across all canvases:
  Expand               2018 - Q1 2020
  Surge                Q2 2020 - Q1 2021
  Build-ahead          Q2 2021 - Q2 2022
  Reckoning            Q3 2022 - Q2 2023
  Focused compounding  Q3 2023 - Q1 2025
  Reacceleration       Q2 2025 onward


5. OVERLAP RULE

Each initiative has one home canvas. Other canvases may carry one summary node
with a => pointer, but not the detail. If a future peripheral diagram needs that
detail, it should branch from the home canvas.

Intentional overlaps:
  - 01 summarizes every domain.
  - Shop Pay appears in 03 as a dependency, and lives in 04.
  - AI channels appear in 06 as a surface, and live in 10.
  - Flexport appears in 09 as a list item, and lives in 08.
  - Global-e appears in 09 as a stake, and its cross-border role lives in 07.


6. HOW TO READ THE EVIDENCE

  - Node tags and link grades are defined in the legend on each canvas.
  - Reported figures are firmest. Call figures are management statements, even
    when precise. 2026 has no filing in the corpus, so 2026 figures are [S].
  - No claim in the audit reached measured causation.
  - "Stopped being disclosed" rests on keyword searches: treat it as a strong lead.
  - Cohort dollar figures are read off unlabeled charts: treat them as estimates.
  - Logistics revenue and gross profit are estimates: no logistics P&L exists.
  - Transcripts are third-party renderings with visible errors. Where a figure
    looks mistranscribed, the canvas says so.
