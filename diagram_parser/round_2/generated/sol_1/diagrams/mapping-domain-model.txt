KEY RELATIONSHIPS
-----------------

Representation 1 ---- * Revision
Revision       1 ---- 1 MappingDocument snapshot
LineGridScene  1 ---- * GridBox (recursive containment)
GridBox        * ---- 0..1 GridContent
Evidence       * ---- * DiagramNode (through source mappings)
Evidence       * ---- * DiagramNode (through DiagramProjection links)
DiagramNode    * ---- * compacted tree lines (through CompactionProjection links)
Draft          1 ---- 1 pinned Revision until the next successful save

INVARIANTS VISIBLE IN THE SOURCE
--------------------------------

* GridBox ids are unique within a scene; positions, sizes, and bounds are integers.
* A collapsed GridBox must name collapsed content; expanded minimumSize reserves space.
* Only settled Representations are openable and shown by the explorer.
* Projection target project, Representation, and Revision must match the pinned target.
* Saving uses Draft.revisionId as expectedRevisionId, then advances it to the saved revision.
* A Draft is dirty when its current name or serialized scene differs from its saved baseline.
