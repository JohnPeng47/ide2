INVARIANTS
|
+-- GridBox ids are unique across a LineGridScene.
+-- Positions, sizes, gaps, and resolved bounds are integers.
+-- A collapsed GridBox must reference collapsed content.
+-- Expanded containers derive bounds from children and optional minimumSize.
+-- Collapsed boxes ignore the expanded minimumSize reservation.
+-- A DiagramProjection is valid only for its pinned project, representation, and revision.
\-- Saving uses expectedRevisionId and advances the draft to the returned current revision.
