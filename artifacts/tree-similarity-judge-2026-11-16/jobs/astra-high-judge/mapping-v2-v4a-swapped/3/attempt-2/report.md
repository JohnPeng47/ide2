## 3. Verdict

The trees identify substantially the same components: workspace/session ownership, history, controller, editor runtime, domain models, agent editing and persistence. B separates CanvasSession and the editor runtime into top-level branches; A supplies their implied parents, live workspace ownership and semantic mutation/editor integration. B's semantic-model parent consolidates content and interaction models that A divides between history and exploration. Transfer navigation also changes owner from controller to history. Most remaining differences are detail: B expands stores, mutation stages and folding; A expands source panes, pinned selection and explicit publication. Startup restoration is distinct to B. The repository checks were used only to resolve component identity, not to assess either tree's correctness.
