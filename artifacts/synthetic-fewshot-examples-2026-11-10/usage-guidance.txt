Match the actual evidence when choosing labels, depth, branching, density, and relationships. Do not impose all four relationship types, public/private sections, error branches, shared-result markers, or lifecycle phases on every diagram. Shared-result references, retry arrows, and event siblings have explicit meanings; reuse those devices only when supported. Branch order implies sequence only when the labels explicitly say so. These are output-style references, not paired source-to-diagram demonstrations. Treat this set as a pool: choose a small, complementary subset for a prompt rather than always including all six.
