Framing

A tree diagram can relate components in two ways:

- Vertical (compositional): depth means "is part of". A child is contained in, held by, or owned by its parent. Reading down a branch, you move from a whole into its parts.
- Horizontal (relational): components that interact without one containing the other, e.g. a call into a shared service, a notification to a listener, or data produced by one component and consumed by another.

The core component diagram was generated as a mostly *flat* collection: its components sit side by side as top-level peers, even when one is actually part of another, and the relations between them appear only as inline references. This hides how the system is put together.
